## 1. Microwave Oven Kinematic & Electrical Defect Profiles

### 1.1 Magnetron High-Voltage Arcing & Waveguide Flashover
* **Physical Mechanism**: Carbonized mica sheet waveguide cover breakdown under 4,000V DC bias; cathode filament deformation.
* **Acoustic Signature**: High-energy electrical buzz with intermittent sharp cracking spikes at **$100\text{ Hz}$** (doubled 50 Hz power line hum) and wideband broadband bursts up to **$8,500\text{ Hz}$**.
* **Neyman-Pearson Boundary**: $\Lambda(x) > 2.85$.
* **OEM Part SKU**: `MAG-2M214-OEM` / `MICA-SHEET-120`.
* **Cost Standard**: Mica Sheet ₹250.00 | Magnetron ₹1,650.00 | Labor ₹450.00 | Escrow Pre-Auth: **₹700.00** (Mica) or **₹2,100.00** (Magnetron replacement).

### 1.2 Turntable Synchronous Motor Gear Stripping
* **Physical Mechanism**: Plastic spur gear tooth breakage inside enclosed 4W AC synchronous gearbox.
* **Acoustic Signature**: Periodic clicking at **$5.0\text{ Hz}$** synchronous with motor pinion gear rotation.
* **Neyman-Pearson Boundary**: $\Lambda(x) > 2.60$.
* **OEM Part SKU**: `MOT-TURNTABLE-SM` (TYJ50-8A7 AC 220-240V 4 RPM).
* **Cost Standard**: Part ₹350.00 | Labor ₹300.00 | Total Escrow Pre-Auth: **₹650.00**.

## 2. RO Water Purifier Acoustic & Hydraulic Defect Profiles

### 2.1 RO Booster Pump Diaphragm Cavitation & Bearing Wear
* **Physical Mechanism**: Air ingress at pre-filter inlet or calcification of internal eccentric cam bearing in 24V DC booster pump.
* **Acoustic Signature**: Severe metallic hammering harmonic centered at **$640\text{ Hz}$** with high-amplitude 50 Hz vibration bleed.
* **Neyman-Pearson Boundary**: $\Lambda(x) > 2.75$.
* **OEM Part SKU**: `PUMP-RO-100GPD` (100 GPD 24V DC diaphragm booster pump).
* **Cost Standard**: Part ₹1,450.00 | Labor ₹350.00 | Total Escrow Pre-Auth: **₹1,800.00**.

### 2.2 Inlet Solenoid Valve (SV) AC Chatter & Plunger Jam
* **Physical Mechanism**: Mineral scale deposit obstructing brass/poly plunger seat; coil AC magnetic flutter.
* **Acoustic Signature**: Continuous high-speed buzzing chatter at **$100\text{ Hz}$ / $200\text{ Hz}$** accompanied by hydraulic water hammer click.
* **Neyman-Pearson Boundary**: $\Lambda(x) > 3.05$.
* **OEM Part SKU**: `VALVE-SOLENOID-24V` (Quick-connect 24V DC 0.2-0.8 MPa SV).
* **Cost Standard**: Part ₹400.00 | Labor ₹300.00 | Total Escrow Pre-Auth: **₹700.00**.
