## 2. Pine Labs Plural Escrow Life-Cycle Protocol

1. **Pre-Service Pre-Auth Hold (`POST /api/pay/v1/orders`)**:
   - `pre_auth: true`, amount in paise (e.g. `125000` for ₹1,250.00).
   - AcuDiag verifies response `status == "PRE_AUTH_LOCKED"`. If card is declined (402), dispatch is halted immediately.
2. **Post-Service Verification Capture (`PUT /api/pay/v1/orders/{id}/capture`)**:
   - Condition: Neyman-Pearson LRT $\Lambda(x) \le 2.45$ AND Anti-Spoofing == `TRUE`.
   - Plural executes an instant capture and splits funds:
     - 80% to Technician UPI Wallet.
     - 18% GST (9% CGST + 9% SGST) auto-calculated with legal GSTN IRN generation.
     - 2% AcuDiag Platform Escrow Fee.
3. **Dispute Cooling-Off Period**:
   - If customer refuses or skips verification testing, escrow remains in a 24-hour cooling state before auto-clearing.
   - If acoustic verification fails, escrow hold is locked in `HOLD_DISPUTED` for secondary master technician audit.
